The Industrial Reflex

Independent research into edge AI that makes safety decisions in milliseconds on offshore and industrial sites — fully offline.

Laya — Edge AI Architecture

Independent research thesis · Ongoing · Demo videos on LinkedIn

Shutdown valve decision
  • System 1 — reflex layer: a small on-site model triages every field report and alarm in ~35 ms — equipment type, possible gas release with confidence score, permit-to-work need and urgency — then deterministic rules page the control room, log, or escalate.
  • System 2 — reasoning layer: only complex questions reach a local LLM grounded in site procedures, which returns a short step-by-step action plan to the technician's tablet and drafts compliance and incident reports.
  • Offline by design: no cloud dependency, built for low-connectivity platforms.
  • Human-in-the-loop: guidance is advisory; people make the final call. Simulated decision latency under 35 ms versus ~8.5 s typical operator response.
